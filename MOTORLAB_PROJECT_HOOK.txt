PURPOSE
Persistent project-side MotorLab hook. A recognized GeoVision session loads the VERIFIED local satellite first, then checks the central Br174/Chatgpt release at the first safe boundary. Central remains authoritative; the local satellite provides immediate activation and fallback.

MANDATORY RULES
1. Do not require a manual MotorLab activation string in GeoVision chats.
2. Load local sync state/core immediately and synchronize only at a safe boundary.
3. Never change MotorLab release during an active write/build/test/promotion operation.
4. If central access fails, continue from a VERIFIED local/LAST_GOOD release and report fallback when relevant.
5. Show the visible MotorLab startup banner in the first available response.
6. Apply progress percentages, Watchdog lightning/recovery, Resume Recovery and fireworks-success contracts.
7. Br174/Chatgpt is authoritative; the local copy is a synchronized satellite, not a fork.
8. MotorLab must coexist with GeoVision geospatial/POI/AI/runtime engines and never replace or rewrite them merely because MotorLab is active.
9. Satellite synchronization must not alter GeoVision application logic, UI, package/signing identity, Google-card behavior, map/POI logic, AI behavior or lifecycle state.
10. Reconcile repository/ref, checkpoint, pending-action and single-writer state before write-capable work.
